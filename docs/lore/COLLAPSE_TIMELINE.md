# DeadMTL Collapse Timeline

Status: draft canon backbone
Task key: LH-02
Scope: lore planning
Server state: pending
Public claim boundary: no fake live-server claim, no playable custom-map claim

## Purpose

This file defines the first master collapse timeline for DeadMTL.

It explains how Montreal fractures before and after the public setting begins:
"Montreal, December 1993. The city is not recovering."

This is a lore planning file, not a public server announcement and not a playable map proof.

## Canon rules

- Root/source website language stays English.
- The game server is pending, not live.
- Open access is planned, not live.
- Do not claim a playable custom Montreal map exists.
- Do not claim Project Zomboid export or load-test proof from this file.
- The exact origin of the outbreak remains uncertain.
- Use grounded public facts, reports, rumors, and classified gaps.
- Keep mystery where it helps the world.

## Stable canon anchors

The following are stable enough to reuse:

- Montreal, December 1993: the city is not recovering.
- Saint-Paul-Emard is Sector 01.
- LaSalle pressure comes from the Pont Mercier corridor failure.
- Ville-Marie is sealed and should remain dangerous, opaque, and difficult to verify.
- Verdun is contested through pockets, rationing, smuggling, and distrust.
- Canal routes are strategically important.
- Signal Monk begins on 1994-01-10 and is last confirmed on 1994-01-12.
- The Canal Cache is tied to January 1994 and remains unconfirmed.
- The LaSalle Pressure Night occurs from 1994-02-01 to 1994-02-02.
- Gate B remains unstable after the pressure night.

## Grounded vs mythic boundary

Grounded canon may describe:

- barricades
- closures
- pressure lines
- rationing
- route failure
- missing services
- signals
- local watches
- survivor movement
- public uncertainty
- sector status

Mythic or uncertain canon should remain partial:

- exact outbreak origin
- what higher authorities knew
- what happened inside sealed zones
- whether some signals are live, automated, or bait
- what exists underground
- what Westmount knows
- what the Oratory rumors mean
