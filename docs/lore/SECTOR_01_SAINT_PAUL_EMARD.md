## Canon summary

Saint-Paul-Emard is Sector 01.

It combines Ville-Emard, Cote-Saint-Paul, and the surrounding canal/industrial edge into the first readable DeadMTL survival sector.

It is not safe.
It is not fully lost.
It is the first area stable enough to map, describe, and use as the Season 0 entry identity.

## Internal subzones

### Monk Residential

Role:
Recoverable residential area.

Tone:
Duplexes, ruelles, small shops, boarded windows, improvised caches, and local memory.

Threat:
Medium. Navigable with care.

Use:
- early scavenging
- local survivor traces
- Signal Monk
- quiet tension
- false safety

Canon note:
Monk Residential should feel survivable, but never clean. It is the part of Sector 01 where a new survivor can believe the area might hold.

### Jolicoeur Barrier

Role:
Western pressure filter.

Tone:
Improvised buses, fencing, municipal debris, furniture, failed triage, and blocked exits.

Threat:
Unstable. Active cluster risk.

Use:
- perimeter events
- LaSalle pressure
- Gate B instability
- The Jolicoeur Knock dispatch seed
- consequence point if ignored

Canon note:
The Jolicoeur Barrier is not a permanent wall. It is a failing filter. It slows pressure and creates warning time, but it should never be treated as fully safe.

### Canal Edge

Role:
Industrial supply and risk corridor.

Tone:
Workshops, garages, fog, cold water, service lanes, canal banks, and hidden marks.

Threat:
High. Useful loot with exposure risk.

Use:
- Canal Cache
- Gate E limited access
- industrial route markers
- future Saint-Henri crossing hooks
- supply rumors

Canon note:
The Canal Edge is valuable because it is useful, not because it is safe. It should create hard choices: supplies, route knowledge, or exposure.

### Angrignon Fringe

Role:
Open movement buffer and western approach.

Tone:
False calm, open green space, abandoned camps, tree lines, sightlines, and moving pressure.

Threat:
Mobile and unpredictable.

Use:
- LaSalle pressure movement
- Gate B instability
- scouting events
- migration pressure
- western route warnings

Canon note:
Angrignon is not a buffer zone. It is a transit zone. Open ground spreads movement but does not stop it.

### Cote-Saint-Paul Industrial Edge

Role:
Eastern industrial and route-control edge.

Tone:
Rail lines, viaducts, autoroute structures, high walls, service roads, blocked approaches.

Threat:
Blocked / tense.

Use:
- future Pointe-Saint-Charles route
- industrial salvage
- faction rumors
- route-control events

Canon note:
This edge should point outward. It is a later route, not the first safe exit.

### Ville-Emard Residential Blocks

Role:
Residential survival base inside Sector 01.

Tone:
Rows of homes, alleys, local stores, improvised barricades, old neighborhood memory.

Threat:
Medium / fragmented.

Use:
- starting identity
- local caches
- survivor rumors
- neighborhood watches
- small-scale conflict

Canon note:
Ville-Emard gives Sector 01 its human scale. It should anchor the sector emotionally.

## Threat logic

Sector 01 is shaped by four kinds of pressure:

1. Western pressure from LaSalle
   - tied to the Pont Mercier corridor failure
   - reaches Sector 01 through Angrignon and Jolicoeur
   - creates repeating pressure nights

2. Canal pressure
   - supplies and routes exist near water and industry
   - fog, exposure, and limited crossings make movement risky
   - Canal Edge should reward planning, not rushing

3. Southern human pressure from Verdun
   - closed pockets and rationing
   - smuggling and distrust
   - contact may be useful, hostile, or both

4. Eastern route pressure
   - rail, autoroute, industrial corridors
   - blocked exits and future expansion hooks
   - not ready as an early safe route

## Neighboring pressure zones

Status:

Canon use:

## Why Sector 01 is the entry area

Saint-Paul-Emard is the first Season 0 sector because it offers:

- residential survival space
- industrial scavenging
- water and canal boundaries
- route pressure from several directions
- a strong LaSalle threat line
- Verdun social tension
- future expansion hooks
- a believable small area for slow-burn survival
- enough danger without requiring the entire island

It is not a tutorial zone. It is a district that happens to be readable enough to survive in first.
